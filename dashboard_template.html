Team WISEFair Share by Playstyle

Same 100 points. Judged by your own playstyle.

Kills-only scoring ignores half of all players. We discover how each player actually plays, compare them to the best players of that style, and split each squad's 100 points accordingly.

How it works

Clean & profile1.6M players in 500k squads, cheaters and merged lobbies removed
Discover rolesGMM on how each player differs from their own teammates → 8 styles, soft membership
BenchmarkEach role's stat distribution among top-10 teams, on held-out matches
Score two ways60% percentile vs that role's benchmark × role importance, 40% one shared contribution yardstick
Split 100Normalised inside each squad; contrast slider shows the policy trade-off

The problem with kills-only

Share of players with zero of each stat, and how strongly each stat tracks final placement. Kills are a weak signal of winning; survival-type behaviour is a strong one.

Who gets paid: kills-only vs fixed pillars vs ours

Average points relative to an equal split (1.0 = equal share), by role, on held-out matches. Kills-only pays Survivors a fraction of their share; ours keeps every style within a reasonable band while still rewarding the Slayers and Apex players who drive results.

External check

Behaviours that were not used to build the roles still line up with them. Wingmen need the most damage per kill (they soften targets); Rotators swim and drive; Slayers and Apex land the longest kills and streaks.

Importance strength 0% ignores how much each role moves team placement. β mixes two lenses: how well you play your style (1−β) and how much you contributed overall by one shared yardstick (β).
